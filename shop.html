Description

Sahya Wild Honey is gathered from the forests of the Western Ghats in Kerala. Golden and aromatic with a floral, gently warm finish. Every jar holds one ingredient: wild honey.

How to enjoy

Stir into warm water with lemon, drizzle over appam, dosa or pancakes, sweeten herbal tea once it has cooled a little, or spoon straight from the jar.

Storage & crystallisation

Keep the lid closed and store in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. If it crystallises, rest the closed jar in warm water and stir.
