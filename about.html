How it started

Honey we would give our own family.

Sahya began with a simple idea: share the honey of the Western Ghats exactly as the forest makes it. No blending, no additives, nothing but wild honey in a glass jar.

We work close to the source in Kerala and keep every batch small, so each jar carries the character of its forest and its season.

“If it is not good enough for our own breakfast table, it does not go in a Sahya jar.”

— Founder, Sahya
