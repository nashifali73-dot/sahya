Questions

Good to know.

Why has my honey crystallised?

Crystallisation is natural for real honey, especially in cooler weather. Place the closed jar in a bowl of warm (not boiling) water for 10–15 minutes and stir. It will turn smooth again.

How should I store Sahya honey?

Keep the lid tightly closed and store the jar in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. There is no need to refrigerate it.

Can children have honey?

Honey is not suitable for babies under 12 months. Older children can enjoy it as part of a normal diet.
